**Accessing Your Intuit Sandbox Company**

The sandbox company login is not directly linked from the main app page. To get there:

1. In the developer portal (developer.intuit.com), open your app and click the API Explorer tab.
2. Near the top of the API Explorer there is a pulldown menu (showing a company identifier). Open it.
3. At the bottom of that pulldown, click Manage Sandbox.
4. This takes you to developer.intuit.com/sandbox-companies, which lists all your sandbox companies.
5. Click the sandbox company you want. The portal will run through several automatic redirects and log you into that company's QuickBooks Online environment.

Note: the direct URL qbo.sandbox.intuit.com does not work reliably — use the Manage Sandbox route above.
